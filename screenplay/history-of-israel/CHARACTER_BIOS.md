# THE KEEPERS - Character Biographies

## Main Characters

### EZRA (Late 20s, Male)

**Role:** Protagonist, POV character, audience surrogate

**Background:**
- Born in Tel Aviv to secular Jewish family
- PhD candidate in Jewish History at Hebrew University
- Specializes in medieval Sephardic communities
- Losing faith in academia's dry approach to living history
- Received mysterious letter of introduction to "the archives" from his dying grandfather

**Personality:**
- Earnest, genuinely curious, idealistic
- Book-smart but street-naive
- Questions authority but respects wisdom
- Struggles with identity as secular Jew in religious landscape

**Arc:**
- **Act 1:** Seeker wanting simple answers to complex questions
- **Act 2:** Overwhelmed by revelations, questioning everything he thought he knew
- **Act 3:** Guardian who understands preservation requires sacrifice and invisibility

**Key Relationships:**
- **Miriam:** Mentor/student dynamic; she sees in him the next generation of Keepers
- **Cohen:** Initially collaborative, becomes adversarial when Ezra won't surrender the data
- **Weiss:** Mirror image - what Ezra could become if he chooses power over wisdom
- **Sarah:** Peer, potential partner, link to ongoing network

**Visual:**
- Slight build, dark curly hair, wire-rim glasses
- Wears practical clothes (jeans, button-down, messenger bag)
- Transforms from academic casualness to hunted intensity
- Reference: Oscar Isaac in *Ex Machina*

**Voice:**
- Educated but not pretentious
- Asks questions rather than making declarations
- Growing confidence through the story
- Final voiceover reflects hard-won wisdom

### MIRIAM THE KEEPER (Ageless appearance, Female)

**Role:** Mentor, guardian, living embodiment of the archives

**Background:**
- True age unknown (deliberately ambiguous)
- Born into a Keeper family in Ottoman Jerusalem (possibly earlier)
- Trained by previous generation, has trained multiple successors
- Witnessed fall of Ottoman Empire, British Mandate, founding of Israel
- Chose to remain hidden rather than participate in public history

**Personality:**
- Calm, knowing, patient yet capable of decisive action
- Speaks in parables and metaphors (teacher's habit)
- Carries weight of centuries but maintains humor
- Absolutely committed to preservation over power

**Philosophy:**
- "Darkness endures longer than light"
- "True power whispers rather than commands"
- "Invisibility is not cowardice, it's strategy"
- "The portable nation survives because it never stops moving"

**Arc:**
- **Act 1:** Patient teacher revealing secrets incrementally
- **Act 2:** Protector recognizing the threat to her life's work
- **Act 3:** Warrior willing to sacrifice herself to preserve the knowledge

**Key Relationships:**
- **Ezra:** Chosen successor, sees his potential before he does
- **Weiss:** Failed protégé, represents her greatest regret
- **Cohen:** Necessary evil, understands his role but won't enable it
- **The Archives:** Her true child, what she's devoted centuries to protecting

**Visual:**
- Indeterminate age (could be 50, could be 80)
- Simple dark clothing, like a librarian-warrior
- Penetrating eyes that have seen too much
- Moves like smoke through the archives
- Reference: Cate Blanchett in *The Lord of the Rings* (otherworldly wisdom)

**Voice:**
- Measured, deliberate speech
- Every word chosen carefully
- Shifts from gentle teacher to steel when threatened
- Multilingual (Hebrew, Aramaic, Arabic, English, others)

**Secrets:**
- Has multiple backup plans we never see
- Other students around the world (Sarah is one)
- Survived the explosion (post-credits reveal)
- The archives were never just one location

### DAVID COHEN (40s, Male)

**Role:** Antagonist/Ally (situational), represents institutional power

**Background:**
- Born in Haifa to Moroccan Jewish immigrants
- IDF officer, decorated service in intelligence
- Recruited to Mossad, rose quickly through ranks
- Specializes in counter-terrorism and historical intelligence
- Believes Israel's survival requires controlling information networks

**Personality:**
- Pragmatic to the point of ruthlessness
- Loyal to Israel above all else
- Respects the Keepers' history but sees them as obsolete
- Capable of both charm and intimidation

**Philosophy:**
- "The state must be visible to survive"
- "Shadow games worked when Jews were powerless; now we have power"
- "Information is too dangerous to leave in private hands"
- "The invisible hand must serve the visible state"

**Arc:**
- **Act 1:** Professional doing his job, initially cooperative
- **Act 2:** Realizes Ezra holds something too valuable to lose
- **Act 3:** Forced to choose between capturing the data and escaping alive

**Key Relationships:**
- **Ezra:** Sees him as naive civilian playing dangerous games
- **Miriam:** Respects her but considers her a relic
- **Weiss:** Enemy of the state, terrorist using old methods
- **The Director:** Loyal servant, will do whatever ordered

**Visual:**
- Fit, military bearing, clean-cut
- Dark suits when official, tactical gear when operational
- Scar on left hand (combat wound)
- Always assessing, never fully relaxed
- Reference: Liev Schreiber in *Defiance*

**Voice:**
- Clipped, efficient communication
- Switches between Hebrew and English fluently
- Authoritative without being loud
- Cold when necessary, not cruel for its own sake

**Complexity:**
- Not evil, genuinely believes he's protecting Israel
- Uncomfortable with some methods but accepts necessity
- Envies the Keepers' freedom from political constraints
- Ultimately lets Ezra escape (or does he?)

### BENJAMIN WEISS (50s, Male)

**Role:** Primary antagonist, cautionary tale

**Background:**
- Born in New York to wealthy German-Jewish family
- Brilliant economist and historian
- Found by Miriam in his 30s, trained as Keeper
- Worked within the network for 15 years
- Grew frustrated with invisibility, convinced new age requires new methods

**Personality:**
- Charismatic, persuasive, messianic
- Genius-level intellect turned to dangerous ends
- Believes he's the hero of his own story
- Narcissistic certainty that he knows better

**Philosophy:**
- "The age of shadow influence is over"
- "People want to see their rulers, hold them accountable"
- "We've hidden long enough; it's time to claim what's ours"
- "I'm not destroying the network, I'm evolving it"

**Motivation:**
- Tired of advising without receiving credit
- Watched lesser minds receive power while brilliant Keepers stayed hidden
- Convinced Jewish influence would be respected if made visible
- Personal ambition disguised as ideological revolution

**Arc:**
- **Before film:** Trusted Keeper who gradually became radicalized
- **Act 1:** Referenced but not seen, shadow threat
- **Act 2:** Revealed as orchestrating global chaos
- **Act 3:** Confrontation forces him to face consequences of visibility

**Key Relationships:**
- **Miriam:** His former teacher, greatest disappointment
- **Ezra:** Young version of himself, tries to recruit
- **Cohen:** Enemy using him to advance state interests
- **The Network:** Betrayed them to prove he's superior

**Visual:**
- Handsome, distinguished, expensive suits
- Silver hair, commanding presence
- Cultured sophisticate who can switch to dangerous intensity
- Reference: Michael Fassbender in *Steve Jobs*

**Voice:**
- Eloquent, educated, multilingual
- Persuasive speaker, natural leader
- Condescending when challenged
- Increasingly manic as plan unravels

**Tragic Flaw:**
- Stepping into the light makes him a target
- His visibility violates the core lesson
- Pride prevents him from seeing he's already lost
- Dies proving Miriam right about shadows enduring

### SARAH (30s, Female)

**Role:** Secondary mentor, setup for continuation

**Background:**
- Born in London to Israeli expatriate family
- Trained by Miriam remotely (never met in person)
- Works as financial analyst in the City of London
- Part of active Keeper network in Europe
- Activated when Miriam sends emergency signal

**Personality:**
- Sharp, efficient, all-business exterior
- Warm underneath when trust is established
- Less philosophical than Miriam, more operational
- Represents pragmatic next generation

**Purpose in Story:**
- Appears in Act 3 to show network survived
- Gives Ezra connection to larger world
- Demonstrates Miriam's foresight (backup plans)
- Sets up potential sequel/continuation

**Key Relationships:**
- **Ezra:** Peer, potential partner, mutual respect
- **Miriam:** Mentor she's never met face-to-face (distributed training)
- **The Network:** Active participant, knows other Keepers worldwide

**Visual:**
- Professional but not flashy
- Dark pantsuit, practical shoes
- British-Israeli hybrid appearance
- Reference: Gal Gadot in *Wonder Woman* (capable, warm when earned)

**Voice:**
- British accent with Hebrew undertones
- Direct, no-nonsense communication
- Switches to Hebrew when emotional
- More modern in references than Miriam

## Supporting Characters

### THE DIRECTOR (60s, Male)

**Role:** Mossad leadership, institutional authority

**Brief:**
- Career intelligence officer
- Political survivor through multiple governments
- Values results over methods
- Represents permanent government vs. elected officials

**Key Scene:**
- Debriefing Cohen after explosion
- Decides whether to pursue Ezra
- Calculates cost/benefit of accessing Keeper knowledge

**Visual:** Avner Kaufman from *Munich* aged 20 years

---

### EZRA'S GRANDFATHER (Flashback, 80s, Male)

**Role:** Connecting link to Keeper tradition

**Brief:**
- Secular on surface, secretly a Keeper peripheral member
- Gave Ezra the letter of introduction before dying
- Recognized Ezra's potential for carrying knowledge forward

**Key Element:**
- Deathbed scene (opening flashback?)
- "Some truths can only be found in darkness"
- Passes torch to next generation without explaining why

---

### WEISS'S TEAM (Various)

**Role:** Armed followers enabling revolution

**Brief:**
- Mix of ideological believers and mercenaries
- Convinced by Weiss's vision of visible Jewish power
- Willing to kill for the cause
- Ultimately expendable in Weiss's eyes

**Note:** Not individually developed, function as collective antagonist force

---

### COHEN'S AGENTS (Various)

**Role:** Mossad operational team

**Brief:**
- Professional soldiers following orders
- Loyal to Cohen and the state
- Not necessarily comfortable with methods
- Survive the explosion, don't pursue Ezra (Cohen's choice)

## Casting Considerations

### Priority 1: MIRIAM
- Most important casting decision
- Needs gravitas, mystery, warmth, steel
- International name for financing
- Suggestions: Cate Blanchett, Tilda Swinton, Shohreh Aghdashloo

### Priority 2: EZRA
- Younger actor, relatable, transforms through story
- Preferably Jewish for authenticity
- Can carry film on shoulders
- Suggestions: Dev Patel (if age works), Oscar Isaac, Adam Brody

### Priority 3: WEISS
- Charismatic villain audience understands
- Sophisticated, not cartoonish
- Strong enough to match Miriam's scenes
- Suggestions: Michael Fassbender, Christoph Waltz, Mark Strong

### Priority 4: COHEN
- Believable as Mossad operative
- Physical presence, military bearing
- Can convey conflict beneath professionalism
- Suggestions: Liev Schreiber, Oded Fehr, Gal Gadot (gender-swapped?)

### Supporting: SARAH
- Brief but memorable
- Chemistry with Ezra for potential sequel
- Suggestions: Rachel Weisz, Gal Gadot, Ayelet Zurer
