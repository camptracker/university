# THE KEEPERS - Feature Film Screenplay

**Based on the History of Israel series from the Parable/University project**

## Overview

**THE KEEPERS** is a historical thriller that weaves together 5,000 years of Jewish history through the lens of hidden influence and shadow power. The film follows Ezra, a young scholar who descends into the secret archives beneath the Temple Mount and discovers that the greatest empire is the one no one knows exists.

## Source Material

Based on 6 lessons from the History of Israel series:

1. **The Land Between Empires** - Geography as destiny
2. **The Portable Nation** - Culture as portable survival
3. **The Network Effect** - Global Jewish information networks
4. **The Shadow Ministers** - Jewish advisors to rulers
5. **The Dual Crown** - Indispensability and scapegoating
6. **The Invisible Hand** - Proxy power and hidden influence

## Note on Lesson Count

## Story Structure

### Act One: Discovery
- Ezra enters the archives seeking answers
- Miriam reveals the secret of geographical destiny
- Introduction to the "portable nation" concept

### Act Two: Network
- Discovery of global Jewish information networks
- The shadow ministers and their proxy power
- The dual crown paradox revealed

### Act Three: Crisis
- Modern threat from rogue Keeper Benjamin Weiss
- Explosive confrontation in the archives
- Preservation of knowledge through portable means

## Themes

- **Geography vs. Destiny**: How location shapes but doesn't determine fate
- **Visibility vs. Power**: The paradox that true power operates in shadow
- **Indispensability vs. Vulnerability**: Being needed creates both influence and danger
- **Portability of Ideas**: How knowledge travels farther than armies
- **Networks vs. Hierarchies**: Distributed power vs. centralized control

## Genre

Historical Thriller / Intellectual Action

Comparable to:
- *National Treasure* (treasure hunt through history)
- *The Da Vinci Code* (hidden knowledge, secret societies)
- *Munich* (Mossad operations, moral complexity)
- *A Serious Man* (Jewish philosophical themes)

## Character Breakdown

**EZRA** - Young scholar, seeker of truth, our entry point into this world. Transforms from naive student to guardian of ancient knowledge.

**MIRIAM THE KEEPER** - Ageless guardian of the archives. Part mentor, part oracle, part warrior. Willing to sacrifice everything to preserve the invisible empire.

**DAVID COHEN** - Mossad operative. Represents institutional power trying to control ancient knowledge for modern warfare.

**BENJAMIN WEISS** - Rogue Keeper. Former protégé of Miriam who seeks to transform shadow influence into visible rule. The cautionary tale of stepping into the light.

**SARAH** - Fellow Keeper who appears at the end, revealing the network's continuation. Setup for sequel potential.

## Visual Style

- **Underground Archives**: Blade Runner meets ancient library - modern tech integrated with 2000-year-old scrolls
- **Historical Flashbacks**: Brief, stylized glimpses of key moments (Joseph in Egypt, Mordecai in Persia, medieval court Jews)
- **Network Visualization**: Golden threads connecting cities across maps, data flowing through time
- **Light vs. Shadow**: Constant interplay between illumination (exposure, vulnerability) and darkness (power, endurance)
